THE HARRY POTTER

Harry Potter is a globally renowned fantasy series by J.K. Rowling, featuring seven books (1997–2007) and eight movies (2001–2011) chronicling a young wizard’s fight against Lord Voldemort. Following orphaned Harry, his friends Ron and Hermione, and their time at Hogwarts, the franchise is a massive pop-culture phenomenon
